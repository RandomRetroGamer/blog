The idea behind it

About us - why we made this

We are UTRGV cs students who wanted to make it easier for students to connect with one another, especially across different majors. Campus life can feel divided into classes, departments, and familiar circles. I built Campus Connect to give students a shared place to talk, ask for help, and discover what other people are working on.

This project is for students: a welcoming starting point for meeting people outside your usual classes, finding your academic community, and sharing the small bits of knowledge that make campus life easier.

Whether you're looking for advice from another major, searching for a group to join, or just want to start a conversation, there's room for you here.
